# Business demo photography and references

The Meridian, Forma, Verde and Studio compositions are original I&I Studio demos.
The businesses, projects, services, products and statistics are fictional sample content.

Research reviewed on 2026-10-06:

- [Horoshop design gallery](https://horoshop.ua/ua/design/): distinct visual identities and a direct path from a design card to a live demo.
- [CatchShop live demo](https://design172.horoshop.ua/): separate catalogue, product, company, journal and contact pages; visible categories, product search and a shopping bag.
- [Horoshop customer examples](https://horoshop.ua/ua/clients/).

These patterns informed navigation and catalogue behaviour. No competitor markup, branding, screenshots or product content is included.
