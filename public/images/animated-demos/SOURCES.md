# Animated design directions, 9 October 2026

Drift, Frequency, Vellum and Canopy are original fictional concepts. Their names,
copy, book covers and network graphics are illustrative, not client work or claims
about real services. The Frequency visualiser plays no audio.

Research references:

- [Awwwards travel reference: Trawelt](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/trawelt): travel direction and expressive art direction.
- [Awwwards editorial gallery](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/blogs/): Remote Control Records (Honorable Mention, May 2025), Seasoned and Good is the New Cool (Site of the Day, 2025). Used as references for music, editorial and purpose-led visual directions.
- [Only A studio](https://onlya.studio/about/): editorial strategy and considered publishing.

These references informed broad directions; no competitor layouts, code, brands
or images are copied. All four use the existing Flow demo navigation, preview
toolbar, form and reduced-motion handling.
